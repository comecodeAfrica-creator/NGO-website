Who We Are

More Than an NGO —
A Movement

The Peace Alliance Network (PAN) Foundation is a value-based peacebuilding organization rooted in Nigeria's rich and diverse cultural landscape. We exist because we believe that ordinary Nigerians hold extraordinary power to build peace — from their streets, markets, mosques, churches, and community halls.

We work directly with victims of violent conflict, bringing together voices from every corner of society to make change that endures the test of time. We do not stand on the sidelines of Nigeria's story — we are active participants in shaping a more just and peaceful future.

Our approach is community-led, culturally sensitive, and deeply human. We engage all members of the human family — regardless of faith, tribe, or background — as contributors to building a culture of peace in Nigeria and beyond.

"Peace is not the absence of conflict, but the presence of justice and the courage to pursue it together."
— PAN Foundation Founding Principle

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

🔭 Our Vision
"A society where peace, justice, and human rights are maintained, irrespective of tongue, tribe and religion."

We imagine a Nigeria — and a world — where the accidents of birth never determine whether you live in peace or in fear. Every community dialogue, every youth we reintegrate, every conflict we help resolve moves us one step closer to that horizon. We are not naive about the scale of the challenge — we are resolute about meeting it.

🎯 Our Mission
"To mobilize a societal network of peacebuilders, advocating for policies and practices that promote sustainable peace, justice, and human rights."

We build networks — not silos. Connecting communities, governments, faith leaders, and civil society organizations to create systemic change that outlasts any single intervention. Our mission is to ensure that peacebuilding becomes a permanent feature of Nigerian public life, not an emergency response.

Our Foundation

The Values That Guide Us

Six principles that are non-negotiable in everything we do — from how we run our office to how we engage communities in conflict zones.

🕊️
01
Peace & Non-Violence

We believe in the transformative power of non-violent approaches to conflict. Every strategy, every intervention, every conversation is grounded in the absolute rejection of violence as a tool for change.

⚖️
02
Justice & Equality

Sustainable peace cannot exist without justice. We advocate for equal rights and equitable treatment for all Nigerians, particularly those historically marginalized in peacebuilding processes.

💎
03
Integrity & Transparency

We are what we say we are. We keep our promises to communities, donors, and partners. Our finances, decisions, and operations are open to scrutiny — because trust is everything in peacebuilding.

🌈
04
Inclusiveness & Diversity

Nigeria's diversity is its strength, not its weakness. We actively include women, youth, the elderly, all faith traditions, and all ethnic groups in our peacebuilding processes — because exclusion breeds resentment.

💪
05
Community Empowerment

We don't bring peace to communities — we help communities build their own. Our role is to equip, support, and amplify local peacebuilders, not to replace or override them.

📋
06
Accountability

We hold ourselves to the highest standards of organizational accountability — to the communities we serve, the donors who support us, the government that regulates us, and the mission that drives us.

Our Journey

A Decade of Building Peace

2009 — Founding
The Peace Alliance Network is Born

A coalition of civil society leaders, faith leaders, and community activists come together in Abuja with a shared conviction: Nigeria deserves better than cycles of violence. PAN Foundation is formally established with a mandate for community peacebuilding.

2011 — First Field Programs
Grassroots Peacebuilding Launches in Jos

PAN deploys its first field teams to Plateau State following intercommunal violence, facilitating dialogue between community leaders and training local peacebuilders. 12 communities participate in the inaugural program.

2014 — Expanding Northeast
Conflict Victims Support Program Launched

As violence escalates in the northeast, PAN expands its mandate to include psychosocial support for conflict victims in Borno and Adamawa states, partnering with international organizations to scale its reach.

2017 — Youth Focus
Youth Reintegration Program Established

Recognizing the critical role of youth in both perpetuating and preventing conflict, PAN launches a dedicated Youth Reintegration Program — providing skills, mentorship, and pathways back to community life for young people affected by violence.

2020 – Present
Nationwide Network — 19 States & Growing

PAN's network now spans 19 Nigerian states, with over 200 trained local peacebuilders, 10+ international partners, and a growing reputation as one of Nigeria's most effective grassroots peacebuilding organizations.

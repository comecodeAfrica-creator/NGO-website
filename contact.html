How can I volunteer with TPHDI Foundation? +

Visit our Get Involved page and complete the volunteer application form. We welcome people with a wide range of skills — from facilitation and counseling to communications, research, and legal support. Our team will review your application and contact you within 5 working days.

Where does my donation go? +

78% of every donation goes directly to our field programs — community peacebuilding, conflict victims support, and youth reintegration. 12% funds policy advocacy work, 7% goes to capacity building, and just 3% covers administration. We publish annual financial reports for full transparency.

Can my organization partner with TPI? +

Yes — we actively seek partnerships with governments, INGOs, faith institutions, corporations, and universities. We offer joint program design, co-implementation, rigorous M&E, and full transparency. Use the contact form above, select "Partnership," and our partnerships team will be in touch.

How can I get help if my community is in conflict? +

Contact us immediately at [email protected] and mark your email URGENT. Include the state and community name, a brief description of the situation, and a contact number. Our Field Operations team will assess and respond within 24 hours for active conflict situations.

Is TPI registered and officially recognized? +

Yes. TPHDI Foundation is a registered NGO in Nigeria with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) and is in compliance with all relevant NGO regulations. We are also registered with the Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering (SCUML). Our registration details are available on request.

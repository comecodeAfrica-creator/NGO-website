Why Get Involved

Peace Is Everyone's Responsibility

Nigeria's peace challenges are large — but so is its network of committed citizens. TPI was built on the belief that ordinary Nigerians are the most powerful force for extraordinary peace. Every volunteer, donor, and partner makes our work possible.

When you get involved with TPI, you're not just supporting an organization. You're joining a national movement — a network of peacebuilders who believe that Nigeria's best days are ahead, if we build them together.

Building Peace From the Ground Up
Every community dialogue, every youth we reintegrate, every victim we support — it starts with people like you.

Volunteer

Bring Your Skills to the Field

We need all kinds of people — not just peacebuilders. From communicators to trainers to data analysts, your skills have a home in TPI's mission.

Field Dialogue Facilitator

Lead structured peace dialogues between communities in conflict. Requires patience, cultural sensitivity, and strong communication skills. Deployable across Nigeria.

FacilitationHausa/Yoruba/IgboConflict Resolution
Psychosocial Support Worker

Provide counseling and emotional support to conflict survivors and displaced persons. Suitable for psychology, social work, or counseling professionals and students.

PsychologySocial WorkTrauma Support
Youth Mentor & Trainer

Mentor young people in TPI's Youth Reintegration Program. Help them develop vocational skills, self-confidence, and a stake in peace. Work can be done in-person or remotely.

MentorshipYouth WorkVocational Training
Communications & Storytelling

Help us document and share stories of peace from the field. Photographers, videographers, journalists, and writers can amplify TPI's impact to a national and global audience.

PhotographyWritingSocial Media
Research & Data Analysis

Help us measure and improve our impact. Researchers, data scientists, and M&E specialists can contribute remotely to TPI's evidence base and program evaluation.

ResearchM&EData Analysis
Legal Aid Volunteer

Provide pro-bono legal support to conflict victims seeking justice and accountability. Lawyers, paralegals, and law students are all welcome to contribute their skills.

LawHuman RightsPro-Bono

Your Money Builds Peace

Every naira and dollar donated to TPI goes toward real, measurable work in Nigerian communities. 78% of all donations go directly to programs — with full transparency through annual impact reports.

₦1,000
Provides dialogue materials for one community peace session — printed guides, notebooks, and stationery for 20 participants.
₦5,000
Supports one young person through a week of the Youth Reintegration Program — meals, transport, and training materials.
₦10,000
Funds one full peace dialogue session in a community — facilitator fees, venue, and refreshments for community leaders.
₦50,000
Runs a full week of community peacebuilding programming — reaching up to 100 community members across multiple sessions.
₦100,000+
Sponsors a trained local peacebuilder for an entire month of fieldwork — transport, communications, and program delivery.

Let's Multiply Impact Together

Governments, NGOs, faith institutions, corporations, and universities — TPI builds strategic partnerships that amplify peacebuilding across Nigeria.

Government Partners

State and federal government agencies seeking to integrate community peacebuilding into security and development policies.

INGOs & NGOs

International and local organizations working in peace, development, humanitarian response, or human rights in Nigeria.

Faith Institutions

Churches, mosques, and inter-faith bodies that want to use their convening power to promote religious harmony and conflict prevention.

Corporates & Foundations

Companies and philanthropic foundations whose CSR and grant-making aligns with peace, security, and community development in Nigeria.

Why Partner with TPI Foundation?

TPI offers proven field experience, a national network, and deep community trust built over 15+ years — making us a reliable, high-impact partner for any organization serious about peace in Nigeria.

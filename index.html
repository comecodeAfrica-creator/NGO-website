Who We Are

Committed to Stopping Conflict, Building Just Societies

The Peace Alliance Network (PAN) Foundation is a value-based peacebuilding organization rooted in Nigeria's rich cultural tapestry. We work tirelessly to strengthen the value of peace, religious harmony, and cultural understanding among Nigeria's diverse people — engaging all members of the human family as contributors to a lasting culture of peace.

We work directly with victims of violent conflict, bringing together voices to make change that endures the test of time.

What We Do

Our Focus Areas for Sustainable Peace

We work across three interconnected pillars — because lasting peace requires addressing the roots of conflict, healing those it has hurt, and rebuilding communities from within.

Community Peacebuilding

Empowering local communities through grassroots initiatives, inter-faith dialogues, and culturally sensitive conflict resolution. We believe peace grows from within — from neighbors choosing conversation over confrontation.

Our Foundation
Vision, Mission & Core Values
🔭
Our Vision
"A society where peace, justice, and human rights are upheld — irrespective of tongue, tribe, and religion."
We imagine a Nigeria — and a world — where the accidents of birth never determine whether you live in peace or in fear. Our work is oriented toward that horizon, one community at a time.
🎯
Our Mission
To mobilize a societal network of peacebuilders, advocating for policies and practices that promote sustainable peace, justice, and human rights.
We don't work alone. We build networks — connecting communities, governments, faith leaders, and civil society — to create systemic change that outlasts any single intervention.

About PAN

Who We Are — More Than an NGO

The Peace Alliance Network (PAN) Foundation was born from the conviction that ordinary Nigerians hold the key to extraordinary peace. We are a value-based, people-driven organization rooted in the belief that sustainable peace is built bottom-up — not handed down.

Operating across Nigeria and beyond, we bring together religious leaders, community elders, youth, women, and civil society in structured dialogue and action — because peace is everyone's responsibility.

We are not neutral about peace. We actively advocate for the policies, practices, and cultural shifts needed to make a just society — for all Nigerians, regardless of tongue, tribe, or religion.
